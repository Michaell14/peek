# EmptyState

What a tile says when it has nothing to show yet: no permission, or nothing added.

- A 40px tinted square with the symbol in `accent`, a `tile-title` line, one or two lines of `hint` in `ink-2`, and at most one `accent` button.
- Say what the tile will show and what is needed: “Show your calendar here. It stays on this Mac.”
- An empty note shows its placeholder in `ink-3`: “Type anything. It stays here.”
